<Callout kind="note">
  This build does not carry a Windows code-signing certificate yet, so Windows shows a blue warning screen on the first install. That is the normal Windows procedure for software without a registered publisher signature (Microsoft calls it an "unknown publisher"), not a sign of a problem.
</Callout>

<Steps>

<Step n={1} id="download" title="Download the installer">

Download `DocOps Setup 1.0.0.exe` from the Download page. It runs on any 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 computer; unlike macOS there is no chip to choose.

</Step>

<Step n={2} id="smartscreen" title='Windows blocks it: "Windows protected your PC"'>

Double-click the **.exe** you downloaded. Windows Defender SmartScreen shows a blue dialog titled **"Windows protected your PC"** with a smaller line saying it does not recognise this app.

1. Click **"More info"**, just below the description; it is small and easy to miss.
2. The dialog now shows the file name and a **"Run anyway"** button. Click it.

The reason: the current build is not code-signed, so Windows has no way to confirm who published it and asks you first by default. It is not a virus and not a broken installer.

</Step>

<Step n={3} id="installer" title="The installer">

The **DocOps Setup** installer opens and asks for a folder. Keep the default and click through until it finishes; nothing needs changing.

</Step>

<Step n={4} id="activate" title="Open DocOps and activate">

Open DocOps from the Start menu or the new shortcut. The first screen is **Activation**: enter the key Agentra issued to your institution, in the form `DOCOPS-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`, and click **Activate**. An internet connection is required.

No key yet? On this screen choose **No key yet (new registration)**, enter your institution and a contact, and Agentra will review the request and email you a key. Once activated you can ingest documents, search, review and draft.

</Step>

<Step n={5} id="updates" title="Getting new versions">

No need to download the .exe again. DocOps checks for and downloads new versions itself:

1. Open **Settings** in the app and choose the **Version** tab.
2. Click **Check for updates**.
3. If a new version is available, click **Download and install** and wait for the progress bar to finish.
4. Click **Restart to install**. DocOps closes, installs the new version and reopens.

<Callout kind="info">
  Drafting something? Save it before clicking "Restart to install": that step closes the app immediately.
</Callout>

</Step>

<Step n={6} id="other-errors" title="Other warnings">

Windows may also show a warning from antivirus software installed on the computer (a third-party firewall or antivirus). Handle it like step 2: allow it to run once. If the app does not open or shows an error unlike anything above, take a screenshot and note which step you were on, what you clicked and the exact message, plus your Windows version (Settings, System, About). Send it to [info@agentra.io.vn](mailto:info@agentra.io.vn) or use **Feedback** inside the app.

</Step>

</Steps>
